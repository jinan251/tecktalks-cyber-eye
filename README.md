# CyberEye
Protect yourself online in seconds! is a cybersecurity detection app that checks URLs, websites, links, and phone numbers to see if they're safe, phishing, stolen, or suspicious

  # what it does
  Detects phishing and malicious links 
  Verifies phone numbers for fraud or theft
  Alerts users about risky content immediately
  safe, no virus or malware 100%

  # why it matters?
  The internet is full of scams and online threats. CyberEye helps users stay one step ahead and browse safely without worrying about fraud or hacking

  # Teck Stack
  Frontend: HTML. CSS, JavaScript
  Backend: Python, Node.js, PHP
  Database: JSON, SQLite
  APIs: Google Safe Browsing, VirusTotal, Phone validation APIs

  # How it works?
  1.Enter a URL, Link, or phone number.
  2.CyberEye analyzes it in real-time.
  3.Receive an easy verdict: Safe | Suspicious | Dangerous.

  # Future Plans
  AI-powered threat detection.
  Analytics dashboard for phishing trends.
  Multi-language support.

  # Contribute
  Worried about scams, phishing links, or suspicious phone numbers? CyberEye is your smart shield for safer communication.
